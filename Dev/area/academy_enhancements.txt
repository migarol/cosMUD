COMBAT FUNDAMENTALS
Darkhaven Academy Training Manual - Level 1-10

Welcome, young warrior! This manual will guide you through the basics of
combat that all adventurers must master.

LESSON 1: STANCE AND BALANCE
Never stand flat-footed. Keep your weight distributed, knees slightly bent.
A proper stance is the foundation of all combat techniques.

LESSON 2: WEAPON GRIP
Your weapon is an extension of your arm. Grip firmly but not rigidly. Rigid
muscles tire quickly. Flow like water, strike like lightning.

LESSON 3: READING YOUR OPPONENT
Watch their eyes, not their weapon. The eyes telegraph intention before the
body moves. This split-second advantage can mean life or death.

LESSON 4: ENDURANCE
Combat is won by those who can outlast their foe. Train your stamina as
much as your technique. Run the Academy grounds daily. Build strength.

LESSON 5: LEARNING FROM DEFEAT
Every loss teaches. The training arena is here for you to fight creatures
safely. Wolf, slug, naga, crawler - each teaches different lessons.

    Wolf (10300) - Teaches speed and dodging
    Slug (10301) - Teaches patience and accuracy
    Naga (10302) - Teaches defense against magic
    Crawler (10303) - Teaches poison resistance

Remember: A warrior who never falls never rises above mediocrity.

- Master Domick, Combat Instructor

PRIMER OF ARCANE STUDIES
Darkhaven Academy - Introductory Magic

Student, you stand at the threshold of power beyond mortal reckoning.
Magic is not simply words and gestures - it is will made manifest.

CHAPTER 1: THE WEAVE OF MAGIC
All magic draws from the Weave, an invisible tapestry of mystic energy that
permeates all things. When you cast a spell, you pull at this thread and
reshape reality itself.

CHAPTER 2: SCHOOLS OF MAGIC
The eight schools each approach the Weave differently:

    - Evocation: Raw energy. Fire, lightning, force.
    - Abjuration: Protection. Shields, wards, banishment.
    - Conjuration: Creation. Summon allies, create objects.
    - Transmutation: Change. Alter form, enhance abilities.
    - Illusion: Deception. Trick the senses.
    - Enchantment: Control. Charm, sleep, dominate.
    - Divination: Knowledge. Scry, detect, identify.
    - Necromancy: Death. Drain life, raise undead.

CHAPTER 3: MANA MANAGEMENT
Your mana pool is limited. A foolish mage burns all their power on the first
enemy and dies to the second. Conservation is survival.

Start with Magic Missile (simple, reliable).
Progress to Burning Hands (area effect).
Master Fireball when ready (devastating but costly).

CHAPTER 4: SPELLBOOK DISCIPLINE
A mage without their spellbook is defenseless. Protect it at all costs.
Memorize spells you'll need. Prepare for variety of situations.

CHAPTER 5: ETHICAL CONSIDERATIONS
Magic is power. Power corrupts. Watch yourself for signs of hubris.
The dark path is seductive (see: Argon, now leader of Order of Chaos).

Do not become what you fight against.

- Instructor's Note: See Advanced Arcana (restricted to level 20+)

THE SHADOW'S PATH
Darkhaven Academy - Rogue Training

INTRODUCTION:
Not all battles are won with steel and sorcery. Sometimes, victory belongs
to those who are never seen.

LESSON 1: MOVING IN SILENCE
Your footfalls must be silent as falling snow. Practice on different surfaces.
Stone, wood, grass, gravel - each requires different technique.

Exercise: Walk the Academy halls at night. If a guard sees you, start over.

LESSON 2: PICKING LOCKS
A lock is a puzzle made of metal. Learn to listen to the tumblers. Feel the
resistance. Each lock has a weakness - find it.

(Practice locks available in training room 10450)

LESSON 3: FINDING TRAPS
Adventurers die more often to traps than monsters. Look for:
    - Discolored stones (pressure plates)
    - Strange seams in walls (dart traps)
    - Tripwires (poison needle traps)
    - Unnaturally clean floors (pit traps)

If it looks too good to be true, it probably is.

LESSON 4: BACKSTABBING
Strike from behind, swiftly and silently. Target the kidneys or base of skull.
One perfect strike is worth a hundred sloppy ones.

WARNING: This is for monsters and PK-flagged enemies only. Murder of
innocent NPCs is forbidden and will result in expulsion and jail time.

LESSON 5: ETHICAL THIEVERY
Yes, there is such a thing. We are not bandits or murderers. We are specialists
in stealth operations, infiltration, and intelligence gathering.

The Guild of Thieves has rules. Break them, and you'll wish you'd just gone
to jail.

Remember: The best thief is one who's never caught.

- From the Office of the Guildmaster

CHRONICLES OF DARKHAVEN ACADEMY
Est. Year 467 - 45 Years of Excellence

FOUNDING:
In Year 467, following the great losses in the Dragon Wars, Arch-Mage Rennard
recognized a critical need: trained adventurers. Too many eager youths died
in their first dungeon, lacking even basic combat knowledge.

Thus, Darkhaven Academy was born.

EARLY YEARS (467-480):
The first instructors were veterans of the Dragon Wars:
- Domick "Ironwall" Varthane - Combat Master (still teaching!)
- Sage Meloreth - Arcane Studies (deceased 489, dragon attack)
- Whisper - Stealth Arts (identity unknown, still teaching)

Initial enrollment: 12 students. Graduation rate: 50%.

THE EXPANSION (481-495):
As Darkhaven grew, so did the Academy. New wings were added:
- The Arcane Tower (485)
- The Combat Arena (488)
- The Library of Collected Knowledge (492)

Enrollment grew to 200+ students per year.

THE NINJA INVASION (Year 502):
On May 15th, Year 502, our Academy was tested as never before.

Sixty to seventy ninja warriors, led by the infamous Snake Eyes, attacked
Darkhaven. Our advanced students joined the RDAF and Rangers in defense.

Casualties were heavy. Twenty-three Academy students fell that night.

Their names are engraved in the Memorial Hall:

[A list of 23 names follows, including familiar ones like "Kethros" and "Sera"]

THE PRESENT (Year 512):
Today, we train hundreds. Our graduates serve in every major organization:
RDAF, Ranger Guild, Mage Tower, Temple of Light, even (reluctantly) the
Order of Chaos.

Our mission remains unchanged: Transform eager novices into capable heroes.

NOTABLE ALUMNI:
- Argon (Order of Chaos leader) - Class of 495
- Aella (RDAF Commander) - Class of 498
- Athmoz (RDAF Intelligence) - Class of 500
- Metheus (Independent Hero) - Class of 499

[Note: The Academy takes no responsibility for Argon's turn to evil]

May the next generation be worthy of their predecessors.

- Headmaster Thalorin, Year 512

RESTRICTED - HEADMASTER APPROVAL REQUIRED

ADVANCED COMBAT TECHNIQUES
For Masters Only

WARNING: The techniques described herein are dangerous. Improper execution
can result in death - yours or your allies'. Proceed with caution.

TECHNIQUE 1: MANA BURN
By overloading your weapon with raw mana, you can create a devastating strike
that bypasses physical armor. However, this drains your entire mana pool and
leaves you vulnerable for 30 seconds.

Use only as a finishing move or in desperation.

TECHNIQUE 2: SOUL TETHER
Link your life force to an ally's. Damage is shared between you. This can
save a dying companion but may kill you both if not carefully managed.

Requires absolute trust. Never use in PvP.

TECHNIQUE 3: CRITICAL STRIKE MEDITATION
By entering a trance state before combat, you can increase critical hit chance
by 50% for your first three attacks. However, you cannot defend during this
state.

Requires preparation time. Not suitable for ambushes.

TECHNIQUE 4: ELEMENTAL INFUSION
Imbue your weapon with elemental damage (fire, ice, lightning). Each element
has strengths and weaknesses:
    - Fire: High damage, fear effect, but weak vs fire-resistant foes
    - Ice: Slowing effect, good control, but weak vs cold creatures
    - Lightning: Chain damage to nearby enemies, risky in water

TECHNIQUE 5: THE BERSERKER'S RAGE
Tap into primal fury. Triple damage output, immunity to pain, but you cannot
distinguish friend from foe. Duration: 60 seconds, then collapse.

ONLY use when alone or your party is safely away.

These techniques separate masters from students. Use wisely.

- Classified, Headmaster's Office
